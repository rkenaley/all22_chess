Welcome to All-22 Chess

The All-22 Chess Tactical Kinematic Simulator is an authentic NFL offensive coordinator workstation. Unlike arcade video games or static play diagrams, this simulator computes continuous 20 Hz explicit physics (50 millisecond timesteps), pad-on-pad trench collisions, cleat-turf traction steering limits (≤ 1.60 Gs), dynamic zone route squeezing, pre-snap motion velocity, and 3D pigskin ball trajectories.

STEP 1

PICK CALL & FRONT

Select an offensive concept from the Situational Call Sheet and match against an NFL defensive shell (e.g., Cover 3, Quarters, Dime Mug).

STEP 2

GATHER QB INTEL

Trigger Jet Motion with J. Watch whether the defense trails in Man or bumps in Zone, and check the Box Count.

STEP 3

ID THE MIKE

Press I to declare the Mike LB. The 5-man line automatically sets its half-slide, and the RB locks his dual-read scan.

STEP 4

RUN & SCRUB

Press Space to execute at 20 Hz, or step frame-by-frame with S to analyze pad contact and route breaks.

FUNCTIONALITY 01

The All-22 Tactical Field & 20 Hz Timeline

PHYSICS ENGINE

The main viewport renders the field from an overhead "All-22" camera angle. Offensive players are represented as cyan discs, and defensive players as crimson/red discs. The live engine evaluates true physical collisions every 50 milliseconds (80 ticks = 4.0 seconds per play).

PRO TIP: Use Space to toggle playback. Use S to step forward 50ms at a time to examine who wins initial pad-level hand leverage at the line of scrimmage.
FUNCTIONALITY 02

Situational Call Sheet (Down & Distance Curation)

TACTICAL PLAYBOOK

Real NFL play-callers don't scroll through an unorganized 200-play list. The Call Sheet groups plays into high-leverage situational buckets: 1ST & 10 (Base Down), 3RD & SHORT (2–4 yards), 3RD & LONG (7+ yards), and RED ZONE (inside 20).

PRO TIP: Clicking any situational tab automatically updates the yellow 1st-down line on the canvas (e.g. +3y on 3rd & Short, +8y on 3rd & Long), testing whether route cut depths actually clear the sticks.
FUNCTIONALITY 03

Pre-Snap Motion Timing & QB Intel Reads

SHANAHAN / MCVAY MECHANICS

In elite NFL offenses, motion does not jump into a static position. The ball is snapped while the slot receiver is at a full horizontal sprint (5.8 yd/s). Simultaneously, the Quarterback reads the defense’s reaction to confirm Man vs Zone coverage and counts the box before the snap.

PRO TIP: Press J to cycle motion: JET RIGHT → JET LEFT → ORBIT BEHIND QB → OFF. Look at the QB Intel strip: if the nickel corner trails across, the defense is in MAN. If they hand off, it's ZONE!
FUNCTIONALITY 04

Pre-Snap "Mike" ID & Line Protection Rules

PASS PROTECTION

When the quarterback calls "54 is the Mike!", it locks the line of scrimmage protection count: The 3-man Half-Slide slides toward the declared Mike to seal interior A/B gaps. The opposite side plays Big-on-Big (BOB) man protection. The Running Back executes a Dual-Read Scan: if the declared Mike blitzes, the RB stones him; if Mike drops and Will blitzes, the RB crosses face to pick up Will; if neither blitzes, the RB check-releases.

PRO TIP: Press I to cycle the declared Mike. If the defense shows an exotic Double A-gap blitz, re-declaring the blitzing linebacker automatically re-indexes the protection count!
FUNCTIONALITY 05

Route Waypoints & Blocker Customization

TACTICAL PLAY DESIGN

Click any player on the field to open their detailed Inspector in the right dock. You can select from 10 Route Tree Presets (Slant, Dig, Corner, Post, Wheel), 4 Pulling Schemes (Pull Kick, Pull Wrap, Pin & Pull), or click and drag landmark nodes (L1, L2) directly on the turf with your mouse!

PRO TIP: Switch to Route Draw mode (R) or click DRAW ROUTE in the Inspector. Click anywhere on the grass to add new waypoints. Drag existing nodes to alter route stems in real time!
FUNCTIONALITY 06

100-Iteration Monte Carlo Batch Simulation

STOCHASTIC NFL TELEMETRY

Football has inherent variance (snap jumps, hand placement, shed timing). Pressing M executes 100 full simulations with calibrated Gaussian stochastic noise in under 300 milliseconds, outputting true NFL Next Gen Stats: Expected EPA, Success Rate %, Pressure %, and Sack %.

PRO TIP: If your pressure rate spikes above 45% against Double A-Gap Mug blitzes, try calling MAX PROT or shifting the slide towards the overloaded A-gap!
